Rclone Integration

private / personal application – not a public service

This is a private, personal integration that uses the third-party rclone software to access and manage Google Drive files. It allows the account owner to initiate and control access to their own Google Drive through Google OAuth.

The integration is not offered as a public application or service. Only the account owner uses it to access and manage their personal Google Drive data.

What it does

  • Connects to the owner’s Google Account via OAuth 2.0.
  • Provides file-management access to the owner’s Google Drive.
  • The owner decides when and how to initiate access; access is controlled entirely by the owner.

rclone itself is developed, owned, and operated by its respective authors and is a third-party project. This integration uses rclone software but is not developed, owned, or operated by rclone.
