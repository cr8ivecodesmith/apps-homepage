Rclone Integration – Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This privacy policy applies to the rclone integration operated under lebrun.org. The integration is for private and personal use and is not offered to the general public.

Data collected and used

The application accesses Google account and Google Drive data only to provide file-management functionality, as requested by the account owner. This includes:

  • Google account identifier (e.g., email address) used for identification.
  • Google Drive file metadata and content, accessed in order to manage files on the owner’s behalf.

OAuth credentials and tokens

OAuth credentials and access tokens are used solely to authenticate the owner’s access to Google services. They are not used for any other purpose.

How data is used

  • The data is not sold.
  • The data is not used for advertising purposes.
  • The data is not shared with third parties except where inherently required to deliver the underlying Google Drive and rclone functionality (for example, when Google Drive returns file content, it passes through Google’s own services).

Data retention

OAuth tokens and data persist only so long as needed to provide the file-management functionality. Tokens are revoked at the owner’s discretion.

Public access

This integration is for private and personal use. It is not offered to, nor directed at, the general public.
